About

I’m a designer from Salento, in the south of Italy. I started with graphic design and front-end development, and over time my work has spread to illustration, motion and photography.

At Trust-IT Services I create visual communication for European research and technology projects: animated videos, event openings, reports and infographics.

Away from the desk I photograph wildlife and landscapes, and I build small web tools for the things I care about.
